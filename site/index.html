      My loyalty offers

      Only for offers loaded to your own card. Screenshot the offers page in the PC Optimum, Scene+ or Save-On More Rewards app and add them here. One set per app covers all its stores (e.g. PC Optimum covers Superstore, No Frills, Independent and Shoppers). Flyer points offers are already included automatically. You can also add Checkout 51 offer screenshots here: they set your own cashback amount and how many times you can claim it.

        Receipts & price tags

        Snap the same receipts you upload to Checkout 51, a shelf tag, or screenshots of an order in the Walmart app (Account → Purchase history). Prices from stores the nightly scan can't see (Walmart, London Drugs, PetSmart, Pet Valu, Costco…) are then included in rankings. They stay on this phone.
